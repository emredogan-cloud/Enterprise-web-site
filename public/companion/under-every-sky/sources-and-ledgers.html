These retellings are not a direct performance of living traditions. They are a comparative editorial compilation drawn from historical and academic sources documented in the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.

This is transparency and context. It is not a legal immunity and is not offered as one. This book has not been reviewed, approved or endorsed by any community, tradition, institution or representative body, and it does not claim to have been.

This page carries the two things the printed book promises and has no room for: every source behind every story, and every factual claim in the book, typed and given a confidence. Nothing here is a summary of the book — it is the evidence the book was checked against.
