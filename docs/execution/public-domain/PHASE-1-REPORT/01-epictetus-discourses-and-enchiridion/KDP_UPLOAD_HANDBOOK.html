05 · Previewer — mandatory

A clean local preflight is not a KDP pass. The Enigmatica edition was rejected for an unembedded font every local check had missed, and this book hit the same defect during its build.

  • Walk the print Previewer page by page. Look at p.176 — the companion page — and confirm the QR prints solid and the address under it is readable.
  • Check the spine text is centred and clear of the folds.
  • Order a physical proof.
  • Scan the printed QR. It must open valicepress.com/companion/epictetus. If the site is not deployed it will 404 — deploy before you print.
