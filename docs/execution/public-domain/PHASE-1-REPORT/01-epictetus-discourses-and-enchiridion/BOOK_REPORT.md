## 6. What the edition adds

- **120 head-notes**, one per chapter, each written after reading the chapter.
- **18 glossary entries** — every one anchored to a passage where Long
  himself supplies the Greek in brackets, quoting his own English rendering.
- **16 biographical entries** — Epictetus names Agrippinus and Chrysippus
  without introducing them.
- **16-row chronology**, saying where the sources disagree instead of choosing.
- **Subject index: 34 headings, 715 references**,
  generated by searching the text. No entry points at a chapter that does not discuss the thing.
- **A concordance to the *Meditations*: 4 verified, 2 recorded as NOT PRESENT.**
  Marcus Aurelius read Epictetus; Long translated both and marked where they touch. Each match
  was made by machine-searching this edition for the words Long quotes. Two passages he cites
  are not in his own selection, and they are printed as absent rather than dropped.

**Two editorial decisions, both disclosed in the Source Note:** the Enchiridion is printed
first (Long printed it last), and the 68 discourses are grouped into seven thematic parts
(Long printed them ungrouped). No discourse was cut, renamed or altered.

**Nothing was softened.** The introduction carries a section called "The difficulties" on the
slavery metaphor, the passages about women, the divination material and the manner; the note
at *Enchiridion* 40 says plainly what is wrong with the argument there.

## 10. Format decisions — made, with reasons

| Format | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct ebook** | **YES** | The core product. 90% margin, and public-domain editions are direct-first by locked house decision. |
| **Paperback** | **YES** | 176 pp is a healthy trade paperback at $3.11 print cost and 41.7% margin. |
| **Hardcover** | **NO** | 176 pp qualifies (KDP 75–550), but a hardcover on an unproven public-domain title competes with established hardback classics series at a price this edition has not earned. Revisit when the paperback sells. |
| **Large print** | **NO** | Stoicism does skew older, which is the argument for it. Against: 176 pp re-set at large-print size runs to roughly 330, pushing print cost to about $5.30 and the list to about $22.99, with no demand evidence at all. Deferred, not rejected — the reason is written down so it can be revisited rather than re-derived. |
| **Kindle** | **NO at launch** | KDP caps public-domain content at the 35% royalty tier and the Kindle store already carries several free Epictetus editions. A discovery channel, not a revenue one. |

**A correction worth recording.** The first build of this book authored its own companion
page inside the interior. It met the numbers, but it was a parallel system: it did not read
the file back, did not decode the QR, did not run the spine arithmetic, and did not produce a
KDP package — all of which the house pipeline already did. `CLAUDE.md` says not to build a
parallel publishing system when one exists, and this was one. It was removed and both Phase 1
books were moved onto the house tool. The interior builders now deliberately produce an **odd**
page count, because the appended companion leaf is what makes the final count even.

The printed page originally promised "a pocket card"; the asset is seven pages, so the
wording was corrected to what the file actually is.
