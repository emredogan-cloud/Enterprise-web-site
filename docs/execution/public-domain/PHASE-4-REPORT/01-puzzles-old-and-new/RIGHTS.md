## Layer 2 — EDITION

First edition, 1893. Not a reprint. The item is the Frederick Warne first edition as
scanned; the title page, the Butler & Tanner printer's imprint and the "By the same
author" advertisement leaf are all present in the scan and all consistent with 1893.

**Modern reprints outrank originals in search** (Article 3, rule 4) — this was checked:
the item's `date` field reads **1893**, the publisher **Frederick Warne**, and the call
number is a Boston Public Library shelf mark, not a reprint house.

**Verdict: CLEAR.**

## Layer 4 — ILLUSTRATIONS / ENGRAVINGS

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Named in the book | **No.** The title page reads only `WITH ILLUSTRATIONS`. |
| Named in the scan metadata | No illustrator field. |
| Count in the source | **462 distinct figures referenced, numbered Fig. 1 – Fig. 521** (59 numbers in that span are never referenced in the OCR, almost certainly figure captions the scan failed to read) |
| Status | **Public domain by date in both markets** |
| Verdict | **CLEAR — but NOT USED. See below.** |

The engravings are clear regardless of who cut them: published 1893, so US public domain
by date; and in the UK an anonymous or pseudonymous work runs 70 years from publication,
expiring **1 January 1964**. There is no live rights question here.

**They are nevertheless not reproduced.** The decision is editorial and physical, not
legal: they are 1893 line engravings recovered from a 300-dpi grayscale scan of a
130-year-old library copy, and they would print as grey mush. **Every figure in the
Valice edition is drawn new, from code.** See `DESIGN/SELECTION.md`.

This means the Phase 3 finding — *a source that does not name its artist is not the same
as an artist who cannot be named* — does not need to be litigated for this volume. No
scanned image is carried into the product at all.

Per Article 3, rule 3 — **source provenance is a rights signal**. This item sits in a
named public library's own collection under `americana`, which carries a library's
determination. It is **not** `opensource; community` (an anonymous uploader's
self-applied mark) and **not** `internetarchivebooks` (lending, not free).

A scan of a public-domain work creates no new copyright in the United States
(*Bridgeman v. Corel*), and no licence is asserted over this item in any case.

**Verdict: CLEAR.**
