# 12-Month Valice Press Roadmap

## Stage 1 (Months 1) — Amazon + website, made real
Fix the launch blockers (`90_DAY_EXECUTION_PLAN.md` Weeks 1–2), rebrand to Valice Press, get the first real purchase working end to end. Success metric: ≥1 real non-Amazon dollar earned.

## Stage 2 (Months 1–2) — Email relationship
Post-purchase capture live, first automations (welcome, post-purchase cross-sell), Codex Enigmatica funnel closed with a real purchasable product behind it. Success metric: list growing weekly from genuine opt-ins, not from any Amazon data (none exists).

## Stage 3 (Months 2–4) — Direct digital sales at real scale
Grow to 6–10 real titles (curated PD + any confirmed-available original titles), each with a proper landing page, sample, and — where the book supports it — its own reader-magnet page. Success metric: repeat-purchase rate > 0% (i.e., at least some customers buying a second book).

## Stage 4 (Months 4–6) — Bundles
First 1–2 bundles live once coherent 3+-title groups exist. Success metric: bundle AOV lift measurable against single-title baseline.

## Stage 5 (Months 6–9, CONDITIONAL) — Subscription/membership, only if justified
Gate: catalog ≥ 8–12 titles AND observed repeat-purchase behavior AND at least one full quarter of direct-sales data to model churn/LTV against. If the gate isn't met by month 9, **explicitly do not build it** — revisit at month 12.

## Stage 6 (Months 6–12, parallel) — Public-domain catalog deepening
Systematic expansion within the A/B-tier categories from `PUBLIC_DOMAIN_CATALOG_STRATEGY.md`, each new title going through the same translation/edition diligence as Meditations. This is the compounding SEO/organic-acquisition asset.

## Stage 7 (Months 9–12, CONDITIONAL) — Institutional sales
Only pursue once the World Games/Myth Hunter/Codex line has a proven consumer track record (reviews, repeat buyers) — institutional buyers (schools/libraries/museums) buy on reputation, and there is currently none to point to. Gate: at least one title with real review/sales traction.

## Stage 8 (Month 12+) — Scaled Valice Press storefront
Re-evaluate the whole business against real 12 months of data: which streams (§`WEBSITE_REVENUE_MODEL.md`) actually produced revenue, which channels (§`CUSTOMER_ACQUISITION_STRATEGY.md`) actually drove traffic, whether the subscription gate was ever met. **Do not pre-commit to marketplace (Model G) at any stage** — that decision, if it ever comes, is a full re-architecture and a different locked-ADR conversation, not a roadmap checkbox.

## What this roadmap deliberately excludes throughout
No multi-vendor marketplace. No hard DRM. No page-based billing product. No subscription before its explicit data gate. No direct print fulfillment build until self-fulfillment or a POD-vendor decision is deliberately made with real volume behind it.
