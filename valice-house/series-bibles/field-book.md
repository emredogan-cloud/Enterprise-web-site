# Series bible — Field Book (sub-series of The Great Book of…)

Screen-free write-in activity books for ages 8–12. Lane A, Amazon print-only.

## Purpose and audience
An 8–12-year-old who has finished the puzzle books that repeat themselves, and an adult looking for a screen-free hour that teaches something real. [O]

## Positioning
Every puzzle is built from something a real culture actually made (Younger Futhark, Inuktitut syllabics, Maya numerals, river deltas); answers are sourced to museums and archives, never invented. A quest structure: regional seals to earn, a completion certificate at the end.

## Design identity
8.5 × 11 in, white paper, black ink, paperback (hardcover is an open question for a write-in book — TEST). Cover: field-notebook idiom, stamps and seals, brown + red seal. Interior art floor 150 dpi was an accepted override — new volumes render plates at 300 dpi (A16). PDF metadata must carry title and author (vol. 1 shipped as untitled/anonymous).

## Recurring structure
120 activities · 6 regions · 22 cultures · 570 page-prints · each activity: what the culture made → the task → where the answer comes from. Answer key sourced.

## Format rules and prices
Paperback $14.99 (net $5.34); large print YES as its own title at $20.23 (net $6.50); hardcover TEST ($22.48, net $5.19). **No ebook, by design** — the puzzles are solved on the page.

## Companion rules
Completion certificate PDF, regional seals, a parent guide; source tag `field-book-companion`. The companion is where the ebook question is answered: printable extras instead of a digital edition.

## Cross-sell
World Myths (same reader) → Field Book Vol. 2 → Codex Mythologica for the older sibling/parent.

## Prohibited drift
- No activity whose answer is not sourced.
- No child-testing claim without a recorded session (vol. 1's `overridden-zero-sessions` stays on record; vol. 2 records real sessions or says none).
- No ebook edition.
- No colour ink.
