# Series bible — Codex

Reference volumes bound like grimoires. Lanes A/B (print-first; direct ebook where not Select-locked).

## Purpose and audience
Readers who look things up: myth readers (28–50, the Fry/Gaiman/Miller shelf), worldbuilders and game masters who need usable, non-cliché, sourced material, and gift buyers who want a heavy, considered object. [O Bestiarium brief personas 45/30/25 %]

## Positioning
Comparative and functional, not regional. Mythologica arranges 76 myths from 19 civilizations side by side; Bestiarium classifies 112 creatures by what they do; Enigmatica is a puzzle book that "carries the Codex name but is a game, not a reference" (K3). A Codex volume always states what it is not.

## Design identity
- Trim **6 × 9 in** regular ($0.012/page), cream paper, black ink, matte cover; hardcover case-laminate; large print as its own KDP title at ~1.75× pages. [O]
- Engraved-line plates in one drawing language; folio-feel cover, gold accent on dark navy/black; serif display type; title ≥ 25 % of cover height, legible at 150 px.
- Four indexes minimum in a reference Codex: tradition, motif, kin-image, pronunciation. [O]

## Recurring structure
Bestiarium entry (the template): name · class · tradition · pronunciation · ~675-word entry · two independent sources · Thompson motif code · cross-references · one plate. Mythologica story: ~1,000 words, unsoftened, comparative arrangement. Enigmatica: 100 puzzles in five gates, three-tier hints, a final answer printed nowhere in the book, verified at `valicepress.com/codex-enigmatica/verify`.

## Format rules and prices
Paperback $19.99–24.99; hardcover $29.99–37.99 (launch together — a gift product); large print priced per page (~$0.055–0.06/page), never by ratio; direct ebook = Kindle list price ($9.99–12.99). Never KDP Select for a reference volume (KU pays ~$2.10 for a full read of 436 pages; and Select blocks direct sale — Mythologica is the cautionary case). Expanded Distribution off (40 % royalty). [O brief]

## Companion rules
Reference index page (short entries + pronunciation + origin, linking to the book), printable cards, and for puzzle volumes the verification/hint page. Source tag `<slug>-companion` / `codex-verify`.

## Cross-sell
Mythologica ↔ Bestiarium ↔ Field Book (young readers) ↔ Enigmatica; Greek workbook; Kwaidan (yōkai cross-reference).

## Prohibited drift
- No game statistics, levels or resistances (not a game book).
- No regional-only volume under the Codex name.
- No claim of comprehensiveness.
- No count in a title that is not measured.
- Premium colour never; standard colour only with a price-engine run that clears 35 % margin.
