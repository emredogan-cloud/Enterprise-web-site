# Series bible — Valice Script

Handwriting and script workbooks. Lane A (franchise, Amazon print-first).

## Purpose and audience
Adult English-speaking beginners who want to write a script by hand before
they speak the language, and who want to know where a stroke order came from
(the Hangul provenance page is the model). [O]

## Positioning
One template, many scripts. The differentiator is not the drills — every
competitor has drills — but (1) sourced stroke orders, (2) a free digital
companion (printable grids, stroke boxes, tracker, later pronunciation
audio) reached from a permanent address inside the book, (3) the cross-sell
into the Valice mythology and classics shelf (Greek → Codex; Latin/Greek →
Classics).

## Design identity
- Trim: paperback **8.5 × 11 in** (large trim, $0.017/page); hardcover **8.25 × 11 in** (KDP has no 8.5 × 11 hardcover). Black ink, white paper. [O Hangul K decisions]
- Interior: one lesson per spread; trace → dot-start → empty box progression; numbered stroke diagrams; provenance page listing which sequences are transcribed from published diagrams and which are rule-derived. [O]
- Cover: flat pedagogical; one large character/letter with numbered stroke arrows; one colour per language on white (Hangul cobalt, Greek terracotta, Cyrillic red, Kana indigo); no QR on the cover.
- Typography: geometric sans for instructions; the script's own reference face for exemplars; fonts must be embedded (preflight).

## Recurring structure (Hangul template)
30 lessons · all letters + combinations · syllable/word building in the last third · 100–125 pages · a "first words" chapter whose word list is **rights-clean** (the Hangul S-0017/18/19 lesson: no CC BY-SA/NC dictionary material; glosses written originally; word selection cited to a public list).

## Format rules and prices
Paperback $12.99–14.99 (run `price-engine.mjs --pages N --trim large`); hardcover $21.99 as a per-title TEST (it adds ~$0.75/unit at 124 pp); **no large print** (already a large trim); **no ebook for sale** (a write-in book — a fixed-layout EPUB may exist for Kindle only).

## Companion rules
`/companion/<script>`: practice grids, stroke boxes, lesson tracker, later audio. Free, ungated, never 404. Newsletter source `<script>-companion`. Printed address text: "Free practice sheets and the lesson tracker for this book are at valicepress.com/companion/<script>. Nothing on that page requires an account."

## Cross-sell
Book N → Book N+1 in the same script; Greek → Codex Mythologica / Bestiarium; Cyrillic → future Slavic folklore volume; Kana → Kwaidan / Japanese myths.

## Prohibited drift
- No dictionary-derived vocabulary without a GREEN ledger row.
- No claims of "learn Korean/Greek in X days".
- No colour ink; no premium colour.
- No KDP series metadata until volume 2 is real (K decision).
- No unverified stroke orders: every sequence is either transcribed with a citation or rule-derived and labelled so.
