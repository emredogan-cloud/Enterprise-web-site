# Series bible — Valice Classics

Annotated, illustrated public-domain editions, direct-first. Lane C.

## Purpose and audience
Readers who want a public-domain text as a book to actually read, who would rather be told exactly which translation and which source edition they are holding, and who value an apparatus (introduction, notes, glossary, chronology, reading guide) enough to pay for it when the bare text is free. [O Meditations]

## Positioning
"Classic material, made newly worth owning." The text is free everywhere; the edition is the product. On Amazon a public-domain book is capped at 35 % royalty; in the Valice store the same edition nets ~90 %, so the series is **direct-first** and goes to Kindle only for discovery, with the `(Annotated)` / `(Illustrated)` tag and a real apparatus.

## Design identity
6 × 9 in, Noto Serif body / Noto Serif Display headings, classic paragraph indents, running heads, real page-numbered contents; emerald/black typographic covers with a single fine engraving (Meditations pattern); original illustrations in the Codex engraving idiom where the edition is `(Illustrated)` (≥ 10 original plates — the KDP threshold — and never a public-domain image as "differentiation"). EPUB is a first-class format here (text-heavy editions).

## Recurring structure — the apparatus
| Element | Minimum standard | Premium standard |
|---|---|---|
| Original introduction | ≥ 1,500 words | ≥ 3,000 words with a "which translation and why" comparison |
| Source note | source edition, translator, year, archive id (PG/IA), what was removed (PG header, footnote markers) | + a provenance page on the site |
| Glossary or chronology | one | both, plus a map/diagram where relevant |
| Notes | none required | section notes |
| Typesetting | fresh, canonical section structure verified (Meditations: 17·17·16·51·36·59·75·61·42·38·39·36) | same |
| Cover | original | original, series-consistent |
| Original share of words | ≥ 20 % | ≥ 35 % |

`differentiation.mjs` measures the original share; the number goes in `project_config.json → measured`.

## Rights
Work, translation, illustrations and apparatus are separate ledger rows. GREEN = US publication before 1931 **and** translator/illustrator death + 70 years verified for the target markets. Project Gutenberg header, licence and trademark are stripped; Standard Ebooks covers are re-verified before use; Internet Archive items are checked for `possible-copyright-status` and for the OCR engine (`ocr` field) before their text is trusted.

## Format rules and prices
Direct PDF + EPUB $7.99–9.99 (premium apparatus $12.99); Kindle later at the same list, 35 %; paperback 6 × 9 $16.99–19.99 once the edition has proved itself; bundles at 70–75 % of the sum (The Stoic Library, Puzzle Shelf, World Play).

## Companion rules
Reading guide, glossary PDF, "which translation?" comparison page; source tag `<slug>-companion`.

## Cross-sell
Meditations ↔ Epictetus ↔ Seneca (Stoic Library); Dudeney ↔ Loyd ↔ Codex Enigmatica (Puzzle Shelf); Falkener ↔ World Games (World Play); Kwaidan ↔ Bestiarium; Werner ↔ Codex Mythologica.

## Prohibited drift
- Never a bare reprint; never Amazon-only.
- Never a modern (in-copyright) translation.
- Never a claim of copyright on the public-domain text itself.
- Never the Project Gutenberg name on a commercial edition.
- Never a page number in a citation that was not seen.
