# Series bible — The Great Book of…

Illustrated, sourced, playable or readable "big books" for families and young readers. Lane A/B.

## Purpose and audience
World Myths: confident readers 8–12 and the parent/teacher who noticed most children's mythology is Greek mythology with a wider title. World Games: families (adult + child 8+ together) and adult game enthusiasts who want to play a 4,000-year-old game tonight and know which rules are attested. [O briefs]

## Positioning
Breadth with discipline: World Myths caps any culture at 4 stories and Greece at 3 (hard CI error, not a warning); World Games arranges 56 games from 39 cultures by mechanic, with reconstructed rules labelled as reconstructions. The subtitle's numbers are verifiable promises.

## Design identity
- World Myths: 6 × 9 in, cream, black ink, one B&W illustration per story, hand-drawn world map, per-culture cards, pronunciation guide, Who's Who. Cover: warm, one hero scene, cream + one saturated colour per volume. Large print disabled by decision K6/A6.
- World Games: 8.5 × 11 in (large trim), white paper, black ink, deterministic vector board diagrams; hardcover 8.25 × 11 from KDP's calculator (never derived). Cover: warm, board motif.
- Young Explorers volumes obey `AGE_POLICY.md` (consequences told, horror not depicted; no added happy endings; `ageAdaptationNote` when a source is softened) and `CHILDREN_WRITING_STYLE.md` (≈950 words, four movements, FK 4.0–6.5).

## Recurring structure
Story: 900–1,000 words + one illustration + a two-line cultural note that must not become formulaic. Game entry: provenance (sourced) · complete rules · board diagram · what is attested vs reconstructed · 1.82 typeset pages measured. Sourcing: two independent sources, one primary/scholarly; retellings and Wikipedia never cited.

## Format rules and prices
World Myths: pb $14.99, hc $26.99, ebook $6.99 (test up from $4.99). World Games: pb $22.99, hc $34.99, **large print $31.03 (net $12.86 — the best print unit in the catalogue)**, ebook $11.99. New volumes run `price-engine.mjs` before a price is written anywhere.

## Companion rules
Myths: pronunciation, culture cards, the world map (the online-map promise removed by K-QR is fulfilled here, and the address is printed only in a new printing). Games: printable board templates and component lists — the ebook is genuinely more useful than paper for this. Kids sub-series (Field Book) has its own bible.

## Cross-sell
World Myths ↔ Field Book ↔ Codex Mythologica; World Games ↔ Falkener (Valice Classics) ↔ World Games Vol. 2.

## Prohibited drift
- No culture over 4 stories; Greece ≤ 3; Australian Aboriginal material excluded and stated.
- No unplayed game shipped as "ready to play" again without recorded playtests (the World Games debt).
- No target number in a subtitle; measured only.
- No QR/URL printed before the page it points to is live and tested (K-QR).
